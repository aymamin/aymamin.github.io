Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to DXF Length Master, a DXF measurement and reporting application designed to help users measure drawing entities and create useful reports and exports.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information DXF Length Master processes, how it is used, and how your information is handled.

Effective date: October 4, 2026

2. Information the App Processes

DXF Length Master is designed to process the information necessary to provide its features.

Depending on how you use the application, this may include:

  • DXF files selected by you for measurement.
  • Drawing information contained within those DXF files.
  • Measurement and filtering information generated while using the app.
  • Information required by third-party advertising services.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or personal documents through the core DXF measurement functionality.

3. DXF Files

Your DXF files are processed locally on your device.

When you select a DXF file, DXF Length Master processes the file on your device to measure supported drawing entities and generate results.

DXF files are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer as part of the normal operation of the application.

DXF Length Master does not intentionally access DXF files that you have not selected for use with the application.

4. Reports and Exported Files

DXF Length Master may allow you to create reports and export measurement results, including PDF and Excel files.

These files are generated locally on your device. DXF Length Master does not upload your exported reports to developer-operated servers as part of the normal export process.

You are responsible for managing, sharing, or deleting exported files from your device.

5. Advertising

DXF Length Master may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google. Advertising providers may process certain information, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising information, and information about ad interactions, in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

The information processed by advertising providers is used for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and improving advertisements, subject to the settings and policies applicable to the advertising service.

For more information about how Google handles information in its advertising services, please refer to:

Google Privacy Policy

6. Internet Access

DXF Length Master may require Internet access for certain features, including displaying advertisements and communicating with third-party services used by the application.

Internet access is not required for the core local processing of a DXF file after the file has been selected and made available to the app, except where a particular feature explicitly requires an online service.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your DXF files or exported reports.

We may rely on third-party service providers, such as Google AdMob, to provide advertising and related services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not intentionally upload your DXF drawings to third parties as part of the normal operation of DXF Length Master.

8. Data Storage and Retention

DXF Length Master does not operate a developer-controlled cloud storage system for your DXF files or exported reports.

Files created or selected through the application may remain on your device according to the storage behavior of your device and the actions you take.

You can manage or delete files using your device's file-management features.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and the information under our control.

Because DXF Length Master processes DXF files locally, your drawing data does not normally need to be transmitted to our servers.

However, no application, device, or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

DXF Length Master is intended as a general-purpose engineering and measurement application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable laws, or the services we use.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
